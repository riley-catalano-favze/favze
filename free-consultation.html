Who Is Favze?

Favze Digital Marketing is a local, full-service digital marketing agency based in Middlebury, Elkhart County, Indiana, founded by Riley Catalano. Riley started building digital marketing expertise at 17, right as Google shifted hard toward AI-driven search, and has since earned more than 100 industry certifications across SEO, paid media, email automation, and marketing analytics.

Favze exists for one reason: local businesses across Northern Indiana and the Michiana region deserve the same digital marketing firepower that national brands pay agencies six figures for, without the national agency price tag or the account manager who's never set foot in your town. Every strategy Favze builds is designed around how people in this region actually search, shop, and choose who to trust.

Favze runs all 12 core digital marketing services under one roof, including SEO, Local SEO, AI Optimization, AEO, GEO, website design, paid search and social ads, email marketing, SMS, and CRM and marketing automation setup, so you're not stitching together five different vendors to get a complete strategy.

And when you reach out, you're not getting routed to a call center or a junior account rep. You're talking directly to Riley. That's who reviews your consultation, that's who builds your strategy, and that's who you'll hear from if something needs to change.

Favze's Proprietary System

The Favze™ Framework

A closed-loop local market ownership system built specifically for lead generation businesses. Read the full breakdown of every stage →

F
Full Findability
Most lead gen businesses are invisible to the buyers actively looking for them right now. Full Findability means you show up everywhere your buyer searches: Google, AI answer engines, maps, directories, and local platforms, Nextdoor, industry-specific sites. Not just one channel. Every channel. If a qualified buyer is looking, they find you first.
A
Audience Architecture
More traffic means nothing if it is the wrong traffic. Audience Architecture builds the targeting structure that attracts your actual buyer before they ever click: the right ad copy, the right content, the right landing page message, all calibrated to your ideal customer profile. You stop paying for leads that never close and start pulling in the ones that do.
V
Velocity Infrastructure
Speed kills in lead gen, yours if you are slow. Velocity Infrastructure builds the conversion system that turns interest into a submitted lead before friction or hesitation kills the moment: conversion-optimized landing pages, click-to-call, smart form sequencing, and instant follow-up automation the second a lead comes in. The faster the system moves, the more leads you close.
Z
Zero Recapture
Most lead gen businesses lose 60 to 80 percent of their leads to silence and inaction. Zero Recapture closes that gap: retargeting sequences for visitors who did not convert, automated re-engagement for leads that went quiet, and reactivation campaigns for past contacts who went cold. Every lead that enters your pipeline is worked until they convert, opt out, or are disqualified. Nothing is abandoned.
E
Exponential Compounding
The most expensive way to grow is to keep buying new leads. Exponential Compounding turns your existing closed clients into your cheapest and highest-converting acquisition channel: review generation, referral workflows, retention sequences, and reactivation campaigns that compound over time. The longer the system runs, the lower your cost per lead gets and the higher your close rate climbs.
The System Logic

Each stage feeds the next. Full Findability brings in qualified traffic. Audience Architecture filters it before the click. Velocity Infrastructure converts it fast. Zero Recapture recovers what slips through. Exponential Compounding multiplies what closed. Remove any one stage and the system leaks. Run all five and your pipeline compounds.

This is not a checklist of tactics. This is a system.
