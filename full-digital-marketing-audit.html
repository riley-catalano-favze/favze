Below is the exact checklist Riley works through on every free consultation call for businesses across Northern Indiana and Michiana. Covering local visibility, technical SEO, AI search readiness, paid media, email, SMS, social, and CRM. Use it to see exactly where your business might be leaking customers, category by category.

01
Local SEO & Google Business Profile
How you show up when nearby customers search
  • Google Business Profile completeness (categories, hours, service areas, photos)
  • Review count, rating, and response history
  • NAP (name, address, phone) consistency across directories
  • Local map pack ranking for core service keywords
  • Citation coverage across local directories
02
SEO & Website Health
The technical and organic foundation of your site
  • Organic keyword rankings for priority terms
  • Site architecture and internal linking
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile responsiveness
  • Meta titles, descriptions, and header structure
  • Schema markup presence
  • Backlink profile
  • Crawl errors and orphan pages
  • Content gaps versus competitors
03
AEO, GEO & AI Optimization
How AI tools find and describe your business
  • Presence in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity answers
  • FAQ and structured data implementation
  • Entity clarity, how AI tools describe the business
  • Content structured for direct-answer extraction, part of GEO
04
Website Design & Conversion
Whether visitors actually become leads
  • Overall site speed
  • Mobile usability
  • Conversion path clarity (CTAs, forms, click-to-call)
  • Trust signals (testimonials, certifications, reviews)
  • Basic accessibility check
05
Paid Search & Paid Social
Where ad spend is working, and where it's wasted
  • Existing campaign structure, if any
  • Ad spend efficiency and wasted spend
  • Landing page relevance to ad copy
  • Conversion tracking setup
  • Audience targeting review
06
Email Marketing
How well your list is being used and protected
  • List size and growth rate
  • Open and click rates versus industry benchmark
  • Existing automation and nurture sequences
  • Deliverability health (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
  • Segmentation strategy
07
SMS Marketing
A direct, high-open-rate channel most local businesses skip
  • Opt-in list size
  • Compliance language and consent capture
  • Current use cases
08
Social Media Presence
Whether your profiles build trust or sit stale
  • Posting consistency across platforms
  • Engagement rate
  • Content quality and brand consistency
  • Profile completeness
09
CRM & Marketing Automation
What happens to a lead after they reach out
  • Lead capture process
  • Pipeline and stage tracking
  • Follow-up speed and automation triggers
  • Data hygiene
10
Competitor Analysis
Where the competition is beating you, and why
  • Top 3 to 5 local competitors identified
  • Where they're outranking you and why
  • Their ad presence and offers
  • Review volume and rating comparison
11
Wrap-Up
What you leave the call with
  • Prioritized action list
  • Realistic timeline
  • Honest cost range
