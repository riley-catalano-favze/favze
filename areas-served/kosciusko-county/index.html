Who We Are

The Agency Built for Kosciusko County’s Distinct Markets.

Kosciusko County doesn’t fit any single marketing template. Warsaw is home to one of the most concentrated medical device ecosystems on the planet: Zimmer Biomet, DePuy Synthes, Biomet, and dozens of related companies anchor a professional workforce unlike anything else in rural Indiana. Winona Lake’s creative and collegiate community sits adjacent to Warsaw with its own character and customer base. Lake Wawasee and the northern lake communities attract seasonal visitors with serious spending power. And the smaller communities of Milford, Leesburg, and Claypool serve rural residents with near-zero digital competition in every category. Every one of these markets has customers searching online who are finding competitors, or nothing at all. We also serve neighboring Elkhart County, Marshall County, and the full Northern Indiana region.

Lake Wawasee and the Chain O’Lakes Economy
Lake Wawasee is Indiana’s largest natural lake. The broader Chain O’Lakes region (Wawasee, Webster, Tippecanoe, and a dozen more connected waterways) draws tens of thousands of seasonal visitors, cottage owners, and boaters every summer. Syracuse and North Webster are the commercial anchors for that visitor base. Every search those visitors perform for restaurants, marine services, lodging, supplies, and activities is an opportunity for local businesses. Most of those opportunities go uncaptured because the businesses serving that market have no meaningful online presence.

Favze is based in Middlebury in Elkhart County, adjacent to Kosciusko County’s northern border.
