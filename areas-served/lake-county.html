Who We Are

The Agency Built for Lake County’s High-Traffic Markets.

Lake County occupies a uniquely competitive position in Indiana. It is the state’s most densely populated county, bordered by Illinois to the west and drawing daily consumer traffic from one of the largest metro areas in the country. Hammond anchors the county’s northwestern edge at the Chicago border. Merrillville functions as the county’s retail and commercial spine. Crown Point serves as the county seat with a distinct small-city character. Schererville has developed into one of the most active residential and commercial communities along the US-30 corridor. A Lake County business with strong digital visibility does not just capture local customers — it competes for regional traffic from across the Illinois border and throughout the entire Chicago Southland. We also serve neighboring Porter County and the full Northern Indiana region.

Lake County’s Economy. Indiana’s Most Commercially Dense Market.
Merrillville carries the county’s largest commercial concentration, with major retail, healthcare, hospitality, and professional services clustered along a dense commercial corridor. Hammond is Lake County’s largest city by population, with deep manufacturing roots and a growing service economy serving dense residential neighborhoods plus significant cross-border Illinois traffic. Crown Point draws buyers from across the county anchored by county government, legal and professional services, a vibrant downtown, and strong local retail and dining. Schererville’s US-30 corridor has created one of Northwest Indiana’s most active suburban commercial environments, with residential density and consumer spending that rivals much larger Indiana cities.
