Who We Are

The Agency Built for LaPorte County’s Distinct Markets.

LaPorte County doesn’t fit one marketing template any more than its two largest cities fit the same economic profile. Michigan City — Indiana’s Lake Michigan port — draws tens of thousands of visitors from the Chicago metro every weekend between Memorial Day and Labor Day. Washington Park Beach, Lighthouse Place Premium Outlets, Blue Chip Casino, a growing arts district, and a collection of restaurants and hospitality businesses serve a visitor base with significant discretionary spending. Most of those visitors research everything on their phones before they arrive, and most LaPorte County businesses are invisible in that research process. We also serve neighboring Lake County, Starke County, and the full Northern Indiana region.

Michigan City and the Chicago Visitor Pipeline
Michigan City is one of the closest Lake Michigan beach destinations to Chicago — roughly 60 miles and a straight shot down the South Shore Line or I-94. The Chicago metro sends hundreds of thousands of day-trippers, weekend visitors, and seasonal renters into the Michigan City economy every summer. Every one of those visitors searches for restaurants, lodging, activities, and services before and during their trip. Michigan City businesses that don’t appear in those searches hand the sale to whichever competitor does. See how Local SEO, GEO, and AEO together close that gap.

La Porte — the county seat — operates on a different dynamic. It’s a county-serving city with a downtown that has seen real investment, a medical services hub, professional services, and a residential base drawing from across the county’s interior. The inland communities — Westville, Kingsbury, Rolling Prairie, La Crosse, and Kingsford Heights — range from a small college town with Purdue Northwest to rural agricultural corridors with essentially zero digital marketing competition in any category. See our full list of services, learn why businesses choose Favze, or read about Favze and Riley.

Favze is based in Middlebury in Elkhart County, operating in the same Northern Indiana market corridors that include LaPorte County. That regional fluency is built into every strategy we build for LaPorte County clients.
