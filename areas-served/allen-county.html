Who We Are

The Agency Built for Allen County’s Range of Markets.

Allen County is the economic anchor of Northeast Indiana. Fort Wayne is not a small-town market. With a metro population exceeding 400,000, a rapidly developing downtown entertainment corridor, three major healthcare systems, a substantial manufacturing and logistics base, and a professional services economy built over generations, Fort Wayne competes in digital marketing channels in ways most Northern Indiana counties do not. Businesses in Fort Wayne face real competition in Google search, and strategy matters more here than almost anywhere else in the region.

Fort Wayne’s Economy. More Complex Than Most Indiana Markets.
Fort Wayne spans healthcare (Parkview Health, Lutheran Health Network, Dupont Hospital), manufacturing and logistics, financial services, a growing tech sector, and a downtown revitalization effort that has added restaurants, entertainment, and hospitality at a real pace. That economic diversity means no two Fort Wayne businesses face the same digital challenge. Understanding the difference between a Fort Wayne HVAC contractor, a healthcare-adjacent firm, and a downtown restaurant requires actually knowing this market. See our Local SEO and SEO services for Fort Wayne-specific strategy.

Outside Fort Wayne, Allen County’s character shifts substantially. New Haven operates as an established city in its own right with its own commercial corridors and identity. Leo-Cedarville has become one of the region’s fastest-growing suburban communities, drawing families with strong schools and newer housing stock. Grabill carries a distinctive Amish and Mennonite cultural identity, anchored by the annual Grabill Country Fair and specialty craft, agricultural, and retail businesses that draw visitors from across the region.

Favze is based in Middlebury in Elkhart County, operating across the same Northern Indiana market ecosystem that connects to Allen County’s western edge. We serve Elkhart County, St. Joseph County, LaGrange County, and the full Northern Indiana and Michiana region. That regional knowledge is built into every strategy we build for Allen County clients.
