The Agency Built for Cass County’s Michiana Economy.

Cass County occupies the southwestern corner of Michigan in a way that shapes its commerce, customer base, and opportunity in ways most outside agencies completely miss. The county borders Indiana directly, placing Edwardsburg, Vandalia, and Calvin Township within easy commuting distance of South Bend and Mishawaka. Cassopolis, the county seat, anchors the governmental and agricultural core of the county along Diamond Lake. Marcellus serves the county’s northern agricultural and residential communities.
