Who We Are

The Agency Built for Newton County’s Markets.

Newton County is one of Northwest Indiana’s most underserved digital marketing markets. Kentland functions as the genuine commercial hub for one of Indiana’s most rural and agricultural counties, pulling customers from across the county for home services, farm supply, dining, medical, and professional services. Its position along the US-24 corridor places it at the center of a county that produces millions of bushels of corn and soybeans annually: a customer base with real purchasing power and almost no digital options close to home. Most Kentland businesses are operating with outdated websites and no coherent digital strategy. The first to build real visibility will own their categories for years. We also serve neighboring Jasper County and the full Northern Indiana region.

Newton County’s Economy. Agricultural Powerhouse, I-65 Commerce, Deep Prairie Roots.
Newton County’s economy is rooted in some of Indiana’s most productive agricultural land. Morocco’s location along the I-65 corridor brings highway commerce and pass-through traffic. Goodland’s position deep in the prairie serves agricultural operators and rural households who drive long distances for services unavailable locally. The gap between what Newton County customers expect when they search online and what most local businesses offer is profound. See how Local SEO, GEO, and AEO together close that gap.

Newton County’s communities share a deep agricultural identity while each serving distinct roles in the county economy. Morocco, near the I-65 interchange, draws highway traffic and serves a community that straddles the prairie and the Kankakee River basin. Goodland, in the county’s interior, is surrounded by some of the most intensively farmed land in the Midwest and serves agricultural operators, rural homeowners, and working families who find their nearest significant commercial options an hour away. See our full list of services and learn why businesses choose Favze.

Favze is based in Middlebury in Elkhart County, operating across Indiana’s rural market corridors with deep familiarity with the agricultural and small-town business dynamics that define communities like Newton County. We understand what drives Newton County customers and how to build digital visibility that reaches them where they are already searching.
