Who We Are

The Agency Built for Berrien County’s Tri-State Economy.

Berrien County occupies a geography that no other Michigan county can claim: the southwestern corner of the state, sharing borders with Indiana and Illinois, anchored by 76 miles of Lake Michigan shoreline, and positioned directly between Chicago and South Bend’s Michiana region. Niles is the county seat, a Michiana-connected manufacturing and healthcare hub whose commercial gravity extends across state lines. Benton Harbor carries the legacy of Whirlpool Corporation and an arts district that has attracted national attention as a model for mid-sized city reinvestment. The shoreline from Union Pier through New Buffalo and Bridgman pulls significant Chicago-area visitor traffic. Andrews University in Berrien Springs brings an international population to an otherwise rural agricultural community. Berrien County is not a saturated digital marketing market. Most categories across the county are effectively unclaimed in Local SEO, AEO, and AI-driven search. The first businesses to build genuine presence will hold those positions for years.

Chicago Visitor Spending. Tri-State Commercial Draw. An Underserved Digital Market.
The Lake Michigan shoreline from Union Pier to New Buffalo is one of the most searched weekend destination corridors in the Chicago market. Visitors search for restaurants, lodging, wineries, and local services before they leave the city. Most Berrien County businesses that serve this visitor market have no optimized digital presence, no GEO footprint, and no visibility in AI-driven search. That visitor spending is real. The businesses positioned to capture it are almost entirely absent from the places visitors look first.

Favze is based in Middlebury in Elkhart County, Indiana, at the center of the Michiana region that Niles and Berrien County anchor from the Michigan side. We understand how the tri-state Michiana market functions, how Lake Michigan visitor seasonality shapes business cycles, how manufacturing employment in Benton Harbor and Niles creates distinct consumer behavior, and how the Andrews University community represents a completely different customer profile than the shoreline tourism market. That understanding is built into every strategy we develop for Berrien County clients. We also serve neighboring Elkhart County, St. Joseph County, LaGrange County, and the full Northern Indiana region.

Berrien County’s Four Winds Casino resort in New Buffalo draws gaming visitors from Indiana, Illinois, and Michigan. The Blossomtime Festival in St. Joseph and Benton Harbor is one of Michigan’s oldest festivals. Fernwood Botanical Garden in Niles draws visitors who search for nearby dining and lodging. These are real visitor flows that represent captured opportunity for local businesses willing to be visible online when those searches happen.
