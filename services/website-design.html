Discoverability Pillars — Baked Into Every Build
08
SEO
Search Engine Optimization Foundation
Every Favze build is engineered with a complete SEO technical foundation so Google knows who you are, what you offer, and where you serve from day one. Local SEO setup is also included.
  • Optimized meta titles and descriptions on every page
  • Proper H1-H6 heading hierarchy throughout
  • Clean, crawlable semantic HTML structure
  • XML sitemap generated and submitted
  • Robots.txt configured correctly
  • Canonical tags set to prevent duplicate content
  • Image alt text and file naming optimized
  • Page speed and Core Web Vitals optimized
09
Local SEO
Local SEO Setup & Optimization
Most of your customers search locally — near me, in Goshen, in Elkhart County. Your website needs to send the right geographic signals. We build that in from day one. Learn about our standalone Local SEO service →
  • NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across the site
  • Google Business Profile alignment and signaling
  • Location-specific on-page content and keywords
  • LocalBusiness schema markup implemented
  • Geographic meta tags and geo signals added
  • Service area pages structured for local search
  • Embedded Google Maps where relevant
  • Built to support and reinforce your local rankings
10
AI Optimization
AI Optimization
Customers are asking ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and other AI tools to recommend local businesses. Most websites were never built with AI discoverability in mind. Every Favze website is. See our full AI Optimization service →
  • Entity-based content structure AI engines can parse
  • Clear business entity signals (who, what, where, why)
  • Comprehensive schema.org structured data markup
  • Authoritative, factual, citation-ready content
  • Optimized for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot
  • AI-readable page structure and content formatting
  • Trust signals and credibility markers AI tools recognize
  • Built to get your business recommended by AI, not ignored
11
AEO
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, and People Also Ask boxes are all answer engines. We build your site to win those placements and be cited as the definitive source. Explore our standalone AEO service →
  • FAQ schema markup on relevant pages
  • Question-and-answer content structured for featured snippets
  • Direct, concise answer formatting Google AI Overviews favor
  • HowTo and definition schema where applicable
  • Speakable schema for voice search optimization
  • Content written to match how customers ask questions
  • Optimized for Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant
  • Built to be the answer, not just a search result
12
GEO
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Generative AI tools — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude — actively cite specific sources when generating answers. GEO makes your website one of those sources. Explore our standalone GEO service →
  • Content structured for generative AI citation patterns
  • Clear, authoritative business statements AI can repeat
  • Multi-graph schema for rich entity context
  • sameAs and knowsAbout properties for entity disambiguation
  • Original, expertise-demonstrating content LLMs trust
  • Optimized for Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot outputs
  • Consistent entity signals across all pages
  • Built to show up when AI generates recommendations
