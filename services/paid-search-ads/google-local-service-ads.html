Paid Search Ads / Google Local Service Ads

Google Local Service AdsPay for leads, not clicks, at the top of Google

Local Service Ads put your business above the Map Pack with a Google screening badge, reviews, and a direct call button. We set up, manage, and tune them for businesses in Goshen, Elkhart, and South Bend.

Check Eligibility Free See How It Works

What Are Google Local Service Ads?

Local Service Ads are Google's pay-per-lead ads for local businesses. They appear at the very top of Google for certain service categories, above the regular ads and the Map Pack. Each ad shows your business name, rating, reviews, and a call button.

You pay when a customer contacts you through the ad, such as a call or a booking request, rather than when someone clicks. That makes it easier to see what each lead costs, and it works well alongside a strong Google Business Profile.

How Local Service Ads Work

  1. Confirm eligibility. Google offers Local Service Ads for select categories in select areas, and some industries require licensing and background checks. We verify your eligibility first.
  2. Build your profile. Your ad pulls its name, reviews, and service area from your Google Business Profile, so we get that listing accurate before anything goes live.
  3. Set your service areas and budget. You choose the areas you serve and a weekly budget, and we set the categories and services that match what you sell.
  4. Manage leads. Calls and booking requests come into your account. We help you respond quickly, because fast replies tend to win the job.
  5. Review and tune. We track lead quality, which calls turn into jobs, and cost per lead, then adjust the setup each month.

Local Service Ads vs. Google Ads

Google Ads is built around keywords and clicks. You bid on search terms, pay for each click, and send people to your website. It works well for businesses that want full control over messaging and landing pages. Our paid search ads service covers that work.

Local Service Ads are built around leads. Your listing sits in a dedicated block at the top of Google, you pay for contacts, and most customers call or book without visiting your website. Many businesses use both, with Local Service Ads for calls and Google Ads for broader reach.

What Makes Your Ad Perform

Local Service Ads reward businesses that look trustworthy and respond quickly. The biggest factors we work on are:

  • Reviews. Steady, recent reviews show up in your ad. Our review generation system helps you build them.
  • Profile accuracy. Categories, hours, and service areas must match what you offer. See our Google Business Profile management service.
  • Response time. Answering leads quickly helps keep your ad active and your leads warm.
  • Local trust. Consistent details across the web support your NAP details, and local links add to your authority.

Local Service Ads Across Northern Indiana and Michiana

Service areas are where Local Service Ads win or lose. We set your ad areas around the towns you actually serve, such as Middlebury, Goshen, South Bend, and Warsaw, and across the wider Northern Indiana and Michiana markets.

Home service businesses often see the strongest results from Local Service Ads. See our home services work for an example of how we approach those markets.

Google Local Service Ads FAQ

What are Google Local Service Ads?

Local Service Ads are paid listings that appear at the top of Google for certain local service categories. Businesses pay for leads, such as calls and booked jobs, rather than for clicks.

How is Local Service Ads different from Google Ads?

Google Ads charges for clicks on keyword-based ads. Local Service Ads are built around the lead, with a profile, reviews, and a Google screening badge, and they appear in a dedicated block above the Map Pack.

Do I need a Google Business Profile for Local Service Ads?

Yes. Your Google Business Profile supplies the name, reviews, and service area that appear in your ad, so an accurate, active profile is required before you start.

Which businesses qualify for Local Service Ads?

Eligibility depends on your service category, your location, and Google's current requirements, which include licensing and background checks for some industries. We confirm eligibility during the free consultation before any setup.
