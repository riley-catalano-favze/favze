01
Strategy

Every campaign is built on real market knowledge specific to Northern Indiana and Michiana — not just picking platforms and running ads blindly.
  • Deep discovery of your business, goals, and ideal customer
  • Platform selection based on where your audience actually is
  • Campaign structure: objectives, ad sets, and budget allocation

We write copy that stops the scroll and produce visuals that perform. Getting a click is only possible when your ad earns attention first — tested across multiple variations.

  • Multiple variations for A/B testing

Landing Pages
Personalized & Custom Landing Page Creation
Getting a click is only half the equation. Every campaign gets its own personalized, custom-built landing page — designed specifically for that ad's audience, message, and offer. No generic homepages. No guessing. Every dollar spent on a click lands on a page built to convert it.
  • Dedicated pages built to match your ad's message
  • Clear headlines and value propositions above the fold
  • Streamlined forms that reduce friction
  • Mobile-first optimization for social traffic
  • Fast load speeds to reduce bounce rates
  • Trust signals: reviews, guarantees, certifications
  • Click-to-call buttons for service businesses
  • Existing page audit and optimization recommendations
04
Targeting
Advanced Audience Targeting & Retargeting
Precision targeting is what separates social ads from traditional advertising. We build layered audiences so every dollar targets people most likely to become your customers — and re-engages those who already showed interest.
  • Geographic targeting: cities, ZIP codes, radius
  • Lookalike audiences based on your best customers
  • Website visitor retargeting
  • Social media engager re-engagement campaigns
  • Customer list matching and upload
  • Strategic exclusions to eliminate wasted spend
05
Management

Active, daily campaign management — not set-it-and-forget-it. We monitor performance, adjust budgets, pause underperformers, and scale winners continuously.
  • Daily spend and performance monitoring
  • Weekly budget adjustments based on results
  • Bidding strategy and cost optimization
  • Scaling winners, cutting what doesn't perform
  • Seasonal and event-based strategy adjustments
  • Monthly strategy calls with plain-language reporting

Complete conversion tracking from day one. Monthly reports in plain language so you can see your ROI without needing to speak marketing fluently to understand it.
  • Platform pixel installation and verification
  • Phone call, form, and purchase conversion tracking
