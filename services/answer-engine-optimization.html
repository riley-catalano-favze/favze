What AEO Is
Your Business Becomes The Source

AEO is the process of structuring your website's content so search engines and AI tools can extract, understand, and display your answers directly, in featured snippets, voice results, AI Overviews, and People Also Ask boxes. Google doesn't just find you. It quotes you.

  • Content structured to answer specific questions concisely and completely
  • Schema markup that signals exactly what each piece of content means
  • FAQ pages built for how real customers actually ask questions
  • Natural language content optimized for voice and conversational queries
  • Authority signals that make AI tools trust and cite your content

The authority effect: Being featured in an answer box signals to every searcher, before they even read your content, that Google considers your business the most credible source. That credibility transfers directly into trust, calls, and conversions.

AEO vs. Traditional SEO

AEO vs. SEO: Why Your Business Benefits From Both

SEO and AEO aren't competing: they're stacked. SEO gets you in the results. AEO gets you featured above them. Here's how they differ and why you need both.

The 6 Answer Surfaces Favze Targets

Featured Snippets, AI Overviews & Voice Search: Where AEO Gets You Seen

AEO isn't one tactic: it's a multi-surface strategy. Favze targets every answer format where Northern Indiana searchers are looking for businesses like yours.

★ Position Zero
Featured Snippets

The most prominent answer box in Google: a highlighted block at the very top of search results. Featured snippets earn the highest visibility of any organic result, including paid ads in many cases.

Example query: "What does HVAC maintenance include?" → Your service page's answer appears in the box above all results, sending searchers directly to you.

People Also Ask
PAA Boxes

Expandable question-and-answer accordions that appear in most Google search results pages. Appearing in multiple PAA boxes for a single search topic means your business occupies enormous real estate on the page.

Example: Search for "roofing Goshen IN": PAA box "How long does a roof replacement take?" → Your answer appears there.

Voice Search
Voice & Assistant Answers

When someone asks Siri, Google Assistant, or Alexa a question, they get exactly one answer read aloud. That answer almost always comes from a featured snippet. AEO is the only way to become the answer voice assistants deliver.

Example: "Hey Siri, who does commercial landscaping in Elkhart Indiana?" → Your business is named aloud as the answer.

Google AI Overviews
AI-Generated Summaries

Google's AI Overviews appear at the top of an increasing percentage of searches. They pull from multiple authoritative sources, cite them by name, and display the summary before any organic results. Being a cited source here is massive brand exposure.

Example: Search "best way to find a plumber in South Bend" → Google AI cites your FAQ page in its overview summary with your business name linked.

AI Tools
ChatGPT & Perplexity Citations

AI research tools like Perplexity, ChatGPT Browse, and Microsoft Copilot crawl and cite authoritative web sources in real time. Well-structured, schema-marked content significantly increases the likelihood these tools reference your business.

Example: Someone asks Perplexity "Who are the best digital marketing agencies in Northern Indiana?" → Your business is cited with a link.

Knowledge Panels
Knowledge Graphs & Rich Cards

Google's Knowledge Graph displays rich information panels about businesses on the right side of search results. Structured data, consistent business information, and authoritative content all contribute to triggering Knowledge Panel visibility.

Example: Search your business name → A rich Knowledge Panel displays your hours, rating, photos, and Q&A directly in the results.

Schema Markup & Structured Data

Schema markup is the technical language that tells search engines and AI tools exactly what your content means, making it dramatically easier for them to select your business as a trusted answer source. Favze implements all AEO-critical schema types on your website with precision, validated to Google's standards.
