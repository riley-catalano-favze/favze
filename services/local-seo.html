Under the Hood

How Google Decides Local Map Pack Rankings

Most business owners are told to "get more reviews" or "post more often" without ever hearing how the pieces connect. Here's the mechanical version of what Google is actually weighing every time it ranks a Local Pack.

Step 1: Crawl & Match

Google matches a search query against every Business Profile, website, and citation it has indexed for that category and area. Category selection and keyword-relevant business descriptions determine whether a listing even enters consideration.

Step 2: Score the Signals

Every qualifying listing is scored on proximity to the searcher, profile completeness, review volume and rating, citation consistency, and website authority. No single signal decides the outcome; it's the combined weight that moves a listing up or down.

Step 3: Surface the Top 3

The three highest-scoring, geographically relevant listings become the Local Pack. Everyone else falls to the "more places" list, which receives a fraction of the clicks the top three get.

Why This Matters for Strategy

A business with five reviews and perfect citations can still lose to a competitor with fifty reviews and a messier profile, because review volume carries real weight. Favze builds every signal together instead of chasing one metric in isolation.
