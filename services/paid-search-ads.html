Google Search Ads
● Live Now

When someone in Goshen, Elkhart, or South Bend searches for what you offer on Google, your ad appears at the top, above organic results, reaching people at the exact moment they're ready to act. You pay per click, and every dollar is tracked against real business outcomes.

  • Responsive Search Ads with 15 headlines & 4 descriptions tested by Google AI
  • Keyword research targeting how your Northern Indiana customers actually search
  • Geographic targeting by city, ZIP, or radius: Elkhart County to St. Joseph County
  • Negative keyword management to stop wasting budget on irrelevant clicks
  • Call extensions, sitelinks, callouts & structured snippets on every ad
  • Quality Score optimization: better positions at lower cost than competitors
  • Full conversion tracking: every call, form submission, and sale attributed
  • Weekly bid adjustments + monthly deep analysis and reporting

The key advantage: A higher Quality Score means Favze can get your ads ranked above higher-bidding competitors while paying less per click. We build campaigns engineered for Quality Score from day one.

02
Platform 02
Google Local Service Ads
● Live Now

LSAs flip the PPC model entirely. Instead of paying every time someone clicks, whether they buy or not, you only pay when a verified customer in your service area calls or messages you directly. They appear above everything else on Google, including regular ads, and carry the Google Verified badge for instant trust.

  • Pay per verified lead only: real customers, not random clicks
  • Appears above all Google results: above the Map Pack, above regular ads
  • Google Verified badge: license, insurance & background checks build immediate trust
  • Full verification & badge setup: we guide you through every step
  • Rankings driven by reviews & responsiveness, great work beats big budgets
  • Dispute management: spam calls, wrong area, duplicate leads all disputed for credits
  • Service area targeting across Elkhart County, St. Joseph, Kosciusko & beyond
  • Monthly lead review, budget adjustment, and performance reporting

LSA window closing: Rankings are shaped by historical performance: the longer you've been active, the harder you are to displace. Businesses that start LSAs now build an advantage competitors who wait can't easily erase.

Platform 03 · New
Apple Maps Ads
● Live Now

Apple Maps Ads launched in the U.S. and Canada on August 14, 2026. Ads appear at the top of Apple Maps search results and in Suggested Places, clearly labeled, with only one ad per search, so there is far less competition than on Google. Apple Maps Ads are built for businesses with a physical location customers visit: restaurants, retail stores, salons, and other storefronts.

  • Search results placement: top position when people search your category
  • Suggested Places placement: shown to nearby people based on local trends and context
  • Only one ad per search: far less competition than Google
  • Shows on iPhone, iPad, and Mac
  • Auction pricing with no minimum spend: pay for results like views and taps
  • Privacy-first targeting: search terms and map area, not personal profiles
  • Apple Business location setup: claimed, verified, and fully optimized
  • Customers can call you or get directions straight from your ad

Live now, with little competition: Apple Maps Ads run through Apple Business, Apple's free business platform. Favze claims and optimizes your Apple Business location, then builds and manages your Maps campaign while most local competitors are still only on Google.

Platform 04
Microsoft Bing Ads
● Live Now

Bing is the second-largest search engine in the U.S., and its network extends to Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, AOL, and Ecosia. Bing also powers Microsoft Copilot search, meaning your ads reach customers across search engines and AI platforms simultaneously. Lower competition means lower cost per click than Google for the same audience.

  • Bing search results + Bing Maps placement via location extensions
  • Extended reach across Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, AOL & Ecosia search networks
  • Reaches Bing, Yahoo, and Microsoft Copilot searchers
  • Typically lower cost per click than Google: more reach per dollar
  • Google Ads campaign imported & optimized for Bing's audience profile
  • Audience skews higher-income, older, Windows/Office users, strong for B2B & services
  • Full conversion tracking, call tracking & monthly performance reporting
  • Bing Places for Business listing aligned and optimized alongside campaign

Your competitors probably aren't there: Most Northern Indiana businesses focus only on Google. Running Bing alongside Google captures a real, high-intent audience at lower cost, with almost no local competition for your keywords.

Know The Difference

Google Search Ads vs. Google Local Service Ads: Which Is Right for You?

Both are Google products. Both put you at the top. But they work very differently, and for local service businesses, LSAs often deliver a lower cost per customer. Understanding both is how you build a smarter paid search strategy.

Standard PPC
Google Search Ads
Best for any business, any industry. Full control over targeting, bidding, and creative.
How You Pay

Trust Signal

Ranking Factor
