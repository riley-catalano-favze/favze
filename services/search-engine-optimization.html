The Foundation of Every SEO Strategy

Technical, On-Page & Off-Page SEO: Favze Works All Three

Most agencies focus on one piece and ignore the rest. Favze builds Northern Indiana SEO strategies that address every layer of the ecosystem, because rankings are earned when all three work together, not just one.

01
Layer 01

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO is everything that happens on your actual website: the content, the structure, the keywords, and the signals that tell Google exactly what each page is about and who it should be shown to. Get this wrong and nothing else matters.

  • Title tags and meta descriptions optimized for clicks and keywords
  • Header structure (H1 to H4) correctly built on every page
  • Keyword integration that reads naturally and ranks reliably
  • Image alt text and URL structure cleaned up and optimized
  • Internal linking structured to flow authority to your most important pages
  • Content formatted for readability, engagement, and conversion

On-page is the layer your Northern Indiana competitors are most likely to have done poorly. It's also the layer Favze fixes first, because it makes everything else more effective.

02
Layer 02

Technical SEO

Technical SEO is the infrastructure your entire strategy runs on. If Google cannot efficiently crawl, index, and trust your website, the best content and backlinks in the world will not move the needle. Favze audits and resolves every technical issue holding your site back.

  • Core Web Vitals: LCP, CLS, and INP brought to Google's passing thresholds
  • Mobile-first optimization on every page and every device
  • Crawl efficiency and index control so Google finds what matters
  • Broken links, redirects, and 404 errors resolved
  • Schema markup and structured data so Google understands your content
  • XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and canonicalization properly configured

A technically broken site cannot rank no matter how strong the content or backlinks are. Favze resolves these invisible barriers before putting effort anywhere else.

03
Layer 03

Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO is how Google measures your authority and trustworthiness based on signals that exist outside your website. Backlinks from credible sources, brand mentions, and citation consistency all tell Google your Northern Indiana business deserves to rank.

  • High-quality backlink acquisition from relevant, authoritative sources
  • Natural anchor text distribution that avoids over-optimization penalties
  • Brand authority and citation building across Northern Indiana directories
  • Toxic backlink identification and disavow strategy for damaged profiles
  • Content-driven link earning through assets worth referencing organically
  • No spam. No shortcuts. No link schemes that create future penalties.

One link from a respected regional publication is worth more than a hundred low-quality directory links. Favze focuses on quality over quantity, building authority your Michiana business can grow on for years.

All three layers working together is what earns page one. Most Northern Indiana businesses are missing at least one. Some are missing all three. Favze's free consultation identifies exactly where your site stands across each layer so you know what needs to happen before a single dollar is spent.

Content Strategy & Authority Building

SEO Content Strategy: Blogs, Pillar Pages & Topic Clusters

Most Northern Indiana businesses publish blog posts and wonder why nothing happens. The reason is almost always the same: content without architecture is content without purpose. Favze builds content that compounds, not content that collects dust.

PILLAR
The Foundation
Pillar Pages

A pillar page is a single, comprehensive page that covers a broad topic in depth. It is designed to rank for a high-volume, competitive keyword and serve as the authoritative home base for an entire subject on your website.

Think of it this way: if you are a home services company in Goshen, a pillar page might be titled "Complete Guide to HVAC Services in Northern Indiana." It covers everything a customer might want to know at a broad level, without going four levels deep on any single subtopic. Those subtopics get their own pages.

A pillar page is long, thorough, and internally linked to every cluster page beneath it. Google reads this structure as a signal of topical authority: this site knows everything about this subject.

What makes a good pillar page

  • Targets one broad, high-volume keyword with strong commercial or informational intent
  • Comprehensive enough to answer the big question, without going so deep it cannibalizes cluster pages
  • Links out to every cluster page covering related subtopics in depth
  • Naturally receives links back from each cluster page, building internal authority flow
  • Designed to be the most thorough resource on that topic in your Northern Indiana market

Favze's role: We identify the right pillar topics for your business and market, write the pillar page, and build the cluster architecture around it. You do not write a word of it.

CLUSTER
The Network
Topic Clusters

Cluster pages are the depth layer. Each one dives into a specific subtopic that the pillar page introduced but did not fully explore. Together, they form a content ecosystem that signals to Google that your website does not just mention a topic but actually owns it.

Using the HVAC example: cluster pages might cover "furnace repair vs replacement," "how often should you service your AC in Indiana," "what causes a furnace to short cycle," and "heat pump vs gas furnace for Indiana winters." Each page targets a specific long-tail search. Each page links back to the pillar. The pillar links to all of them.

Why this works: Google does not just rank pages. It ranks websites that demonstrate topical authority. A site with one great page about HVAC will almost always lose to a site with a pillar page and twelve cluster pages covering the subject from every angle. Clusters are how you build that authority systematically.

BLOG
The Engine
Blog Content

Blog posts are the most misunderstood piece of the SEO content puzzle. Most Northern Indiana businesses either do not blog at all or publish content with no keyword targeting, no internal linking strategy, and no connection to what they are actually trying to rank for. Those posts generate no traffic and serve no purpose.

Strategic blog content is different. Every post targets a specific search query your ideal customer is using. Every post supports a cluster or pillar. Every post is an entry point that brings someone into your content ecosystem and moves them toward a conversion. Done right, a blog becomes the channel that continuously feeds your site new ranking opportunities, new traffic, and new leads without ongoing ad spend.

The compound effect: A blog post published today can generate traffic for years. Fifty well-targeted posts create fifty separate entry points into your website. Each one compounds. That is the difference between renting attention through ads and owning traffic through SEO.
