What We Actually Believe

What
Favze Stands For

These aren't corporate values on a wall nobody reads. They're the specific beliefs that decide what Favze sells, how we report, when we push back, and why this agency exists at all.

01

Buyers, not Followers.

Follower count is the most misleading metric in marketing. A business with 4,000 followers and empty tables on Tuesday has a problem, not a success story. Favze builds for all three buyer intent tiers: high intent, medium intent, and unaware. Because every one of them can become a customer.

See it in practice
Campaigns are built and measured against booked appointments and closed sales, not likes or reach. It's the same idea behind Purple Cow, the book that convinced Riley forgettable is the only real failure. Vanity metrics are forgettable by design.
See the Full Framework
02

Transparent pricing.
Always.

Every cost is visible before you sign anything. No setup fees buried in the contract. No overages billed after the fact. No line items that require an explanation you didn't ask for. You budget for marketing the same way you budget for anything else with confidence in the number.

Every invoice includes a plain-language breakdown of what you're paying for before it's ever sent, not after you have to ask. Pricing gets laid out on the very first call.
